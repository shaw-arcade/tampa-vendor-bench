# Questions for the owner (copy and send)

Hi, I am putting together a list of vetted, fairly priced repair people for your houses. To do it for every area you own in, I need a few things. Short answers are fine.

1. Where are the houses? A list of cities or ZIP codes is enough. If you have an address list you can send, even better, because it lets me group houses so one plumber covers a whole cluster.

2. Which repairs cost you the most or happen the most? Pick from: AC, plumbing, electrical, roof, general handyman work, pest, lawn, turnover cleaning. Or just tell me what bugs you.

3. Can you send me a few recent repair invoices, or last year's total repair spend? Three or four bills is enough. I use them to show you what you paid versus what the vetted people charge.

4. Do you want me to get real quotes, or just the vetted list? Quotes mean I contact the vendors on your behalf. If yes, which email should they reply to?

5. Are there any vendors you already like and want to keep? Or any you never want to see again?

6. Do any of the houses have something I should know about? Very old plumbing or wiring, a roof near the end of its life, a house that always has problems.

That is all I need. Once I have the cities, you get a list for each area with license and insurance checked on every name, their rates, and how they compare to what you paid.
