Florida does not license painters, so there is no state license to look up here, unlike plumbers or electricians. What stands in for it: an active Florida entity, a county business tax receipt, and workers' compensation. Painting counts as construction in Florida, so one employee triggers coverage. Ask for the certificate before anyone starts, not after.
